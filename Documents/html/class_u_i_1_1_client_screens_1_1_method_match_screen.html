Detailed Description

Method match screen goes on the MatchUI object in UI scene. Opens when the player clicks a researcher WHILE a client follows. Matching is two steps:

  1. pick an approach (Quantitative / Qualitative / Mixed), then NEXT
  2. pick one of the researcher's methodologies, then MATCH (CHANGE goes back to step 1) Grading and money are in FirmController.CompleteStudy. NO MATCH or CLOSE just closes the screen.
